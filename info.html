What is in every item on our menu, from espresso classics to matcha and croffles. Pick a category to jump straight to it.

Kape Classics

The everyday espresso drinks. Gamay is the small size and Dako the large.

Rule of thumb: the more milk, the milder the drink. Cortado and cappuccino taste the most of coffee, latte is the gentlest.

Latte

Espresso with plenty of steamed milk and a thin layer of foam. Smooth, mild and an easy place to start.

EspressoSteamed milk

Americano

Espresso topped up with hot water for a long, clean cup that keeps the coffee flavour front and centre.

EspressoWaterDairy-free

Cappuccino

Espresso, steamed milk and a thick cap of foam in roughly equal parts. Airier and stronger-tasting than a latte.

EspressoMilk foam

Cortado

A small drink of espresso cut with about the same amount of warm milk and very little foam. Short, strong and smooth.

EspressoSteamed milk

Espresso (double shot)

Two shots of concentrated espresso served straight. Bold, syrupy and quick.

EspressoDairy-free

Flavored Latte

A classic latte with a syrup, sauce or spread added. Hot or iced.

Spanish Latte
